Introduction

I’m Renjith. I build websites and web apps from Kerala, mostly in Django.

I’ve been at it since 2021. Most of the work happens behind a login: product lists, orders, permissions, the admin screens that keep a shop running. The public pages sit on top of that, written by hand.

I’d rather ship one thing that works than five that half do. If a page needs a framework to be readable, it probably doesn’t need the framework.
